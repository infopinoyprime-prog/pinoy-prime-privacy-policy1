1. Who we are

Pinoy Prime Booking Services is a service-booking application that helps customers request services and allows authorized workers and administrators to manage bookings.

The service is operated by Pinoy Prime Technical Services (“Pinoy Prime,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions, use the contact details in the Contact us section.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use and your role in the app, we may handle the following categories of information:

  • Account and profile information: information such as your name, email address, phone number, login credentials or authentication identifiers, and account role.
  • Booking information: selected service, booking description, requested date or time, service address or location details you submit, booking status, and communications related to a booking.
  • Worker and job information: job assignments, work status, and photos or other proof submitted to document completed or ongoing work.
  • Payment-related information: selected payment method and payment status. If you pay by bank transfer, information or proof you choose to submit may also be processed. Do not submit full card numbers, PINs, passwords, or banking login credentials through the app.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated when the app operates, such as device, app version, diagnostic, and security information, where collected by the app or its service providers.

We do not intend to collect information that is not needed to provide or protect the booking service. The exact information requested may vary by app version and the features you use.

3. How we use information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • creating and managing user accounts and authenticating users;
  • receiving, scheduling, assigning, and managing service bookings;
  • allowing customers, assigned workers, and authorized administrators to see information needed for their roles;
  • recording booking progress, service completion, and payment status;
  • providing customer support and responding to requests;
  • maintaining app security, preventing misuse, troubleshooting, and improving reliability; and
  • meeting applicable legal obligations and resolving disputes.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Within the service: relevant booking details may be made available to the assigned worker and authorized administrators so they can fulfil and manage a booking.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database, and related app infrastructure. Information processed by Supabase is subject to its own terms and privacy documentation.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights.

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for their own unrelated purposes.

5. Storage and security

Information is processed using systems that support the app, including Supabase. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect information and limit access to people and systems that need it.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain booking and account records, handle support requests, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can contact us using the details below to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where it is necessary to resolve an outstanding booking, payment, dispute, or security issue.

8. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Pinoy Prime Booking Services account and associated personal information by contacting us using the email address in the Contact us section. Include the email address associated with your account and state that you are requesting account deletion. Do not send your password.

We may contact you to verify the request. After verification, we will delete or de-identify information that we are not required or otherwise permitted to retain. Certain transaction, security, or legal records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The app is designed for people seeking or providing service bookings and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, services, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
